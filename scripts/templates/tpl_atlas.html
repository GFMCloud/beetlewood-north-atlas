When each group is out

Observations by month of the year, pooled across every season on the property. Counts are printed in the cells, so the shading never has to be trusted on its own.

Row shading answers "when is this group active"; overall shading answers "when is anything happening at all". Insects outnumber everything else here by an order of magnitude, so on the overall scale the other rows go nearly blank — which is honest, but not what you want when you are planning a walk.

What he logs — a revealed interest profile

Nothing here is hand-picked. It is derived from his own logging behaviour: effort (how many observations), depth (how many distinct species), recency (share logged in the last year) and persistence (how many months of the year he is at it). This index is what weights the gap checklist.

Gap checklist

Research-grade species other iNaturalist users have recorded near the farm that he has not logged — ranked by his own interest profile, so the top of the list is "findable near here, in the groups you actually work on".
